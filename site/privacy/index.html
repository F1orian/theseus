Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Theseus is an app for your own Open WebUI server. We don't run a server for Theseus, and we don't receive, see or store anything you do in the app. This policy covers the app and this website.

The app: what we collect

Nothing. Theseus contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it doesn't send data to us or to any third party chosen by us. If you choose to share crash reports or app analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple shares them with us under Apple's privacy policy.

Where your data goes

Everything you do in Theseus goes to the Open WebUI server you connect to, and only there:

  • your sign-in details, profile, chats, prompts, files, photos and recordings you attach, memories, projects and settings stored on the server;
  • if you turn it on in Settings › Permissions, your approximate location, which is added to your prompts;
  • responses you rate or report, with a copy of the chat, for the server's admins.

That server is run by its admin (you, your organisation or someone else), and their privacy policy and settings apply to it. The server may send your messages and files to AI model providers the admin connected (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) and to tools such as web search. Theseus asks for your permission before anything is sent for an account, and shows who the admin is in Settings › Data & privacy.

What stays on your device

  • A cache of your chat list and chats, so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • Your sign-in token, in the iOS Keychain.
  • App preferences such as appearance, voice and permissions.
  • A small snapshot for the widgets and the Share extension, in the app's shared container.

Voice dictation and read-aloud run on your device. If the server has its own speech engine, recordings and text are sent to the server instead. If on-device recognition isn't available for your language, iOS may use Apple's speech recognition service.

The camera, microphone, photos, speech recognition and location are used only when you use the feature that needs them, and only after you allow it in iOS.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Theseus checks them on your device. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account; Apple only gives us sales reports.

Your choices in the app

  • Clear cache (Settings › Data & privacy) deletes the local copy of your chats.
  • Remove from this device signs you out and deletes the account's local data.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data on the device.
  • To access, export or delete data on the server, including your account, contact the server's admin. Theseus shows their contact details when the server publishes them.
